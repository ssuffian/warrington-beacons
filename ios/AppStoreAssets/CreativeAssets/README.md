# App Store creative assets

Created October 6, 2026 for Warrington Talking Trails, on the existing Lions Pride Park listing (App Store ID 1532727572).

## Upload files

- `product-page-header.png`: 3840 × 1646 PNG, RGB, no alpha. Select for Product Page Header.
- `search-results.png`: 3840 × 2560 PNG, RGB, no alpha. Select for Search Results.

These are coordinated conceptual nature illustrations, not geographic maps or photographs of particular trail stops. The central landmark/beacon motif connects to the app icon. No baked-in text means they can accompany any listing language.

Upload these images to the App Store Connect Asset Library and assign each to its matching placement. Use Apple's Preview tool to inspect device-dependent crops before submitting the assets for review. Assets have been created locally; they have not been uploaded or submitted.

Promotional text is in `../Metadata/promotional-text.txt` (139 characters, within Apple's 170-character limit).

## Production notes

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using `../AppIcon-1024.png` as a palette and motif reference. Final PNGs were mechanically resized with macOS sips to the exact upload dimensions; source images are lower-resolution generated artwork. The focal motif is centered with generous space around it for cropping. Final exports were visually inspected and their dimensions and lack of alpha checked locally; final placement still needs App Store Connect Preview.

## Generation prompts

### Header

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: Apple App Store product page header for Warrington Talking Trails.
Create a polished editorial nature illustration, very wide landscape canvas exactly 3840 by 1646 pixels. A gently winding warm golden trail through a Pennsylvania township park, deciduous trees, native meadow grasses, subtle creek, soft morning light. Elegant layered cut-paper-like shapes with subtle fine grain, sophisticated flat illustration, forest green, sage, cream and golden yellow matching the reference app icon. Low rolling ground, no mountains. At the center of the image a small golden map pin with two gentle pale-green radio arcs suggests discovering trail landmarks. Keep this central focal motif and recognizable winding path inside the middle 40 percent width and middle 40 percent height, with calm supporting nature extending to all edges. No text or lettering, no app icon tile, no phone, no logos or badges, no invented real landmark or map, no border. This is an evocative illustration of local trail discovery, not an actual geographic map. Fully opaque. Reference image is for brand colors and radio-arc language only; do not reproduce the app icon.

### Search results

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: Apple App Store search results creative image for Warrington Talking Trails.
Create a polished editorial nature illustration in a 3:2 landscape canvas, target 3840 by 2560 pixels. The illustration immediately communicates self-guided local nature trail discovery: a warm golden winding path curves through a peaceful Pennsylvania township park of deciduous woodland and native meadow, a subtle creek, soft morning light. Flat layered cut-paper-like shapes, restrained fine grain, forest green, sage, warm cream and golden yellow, consistent with the reference app icon. A simple golden map pin at the central bend with two pale-green gentle radio arcs above it symbolizes discovering landmarks via nearby beacons. Keep all key focal content in the central 55 percent width and central 40 percent height; the pin is smaller than the landscape, nature is the hero. Low rolling ground, no mountains or invented real landmark. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no app icon tile, no phone or invented UI, no badges or border. Fully opaque. This is evocative illustrative artwork, not an actual geographic map. Reference image is only for colors and radio arc language.
