Privacy Policy

Warrington Parks & Trails (Android) — last updated 10 August 2026

Short version: this app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect or transmit your location. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server of ours to send it to.

What the app does

Warrington Parks & Trails shows the trails and points of interest at Lions Pride Park and along the US202 to Bradford Dam trail. Small Bluetooth beacons are installed at points of interest; when the app detects that you are near one, it shows and announces information about that landmark.

Permissions, and why each is needed

Location (precise or approximate)
Used to draw your position on the map, and required by Android itself in order to scan for Bluetooth beacons and to keep scanning while the screen is off. Your location is used on your device only. It is never transmitted, never stored beyond the moment it is displayed, and never shared with anyone.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth scanning)
Used to detect the trail beacons, which broadcast nothing but a fixed identifier. The app reads those identifiers to work out which landmark you have reached. It does not connect to any device, and does not scan for or identify phones, wearables or other equipment around you.
Notifications
Used to tell you about a landmark you have reached — including when your phone is in a pocket or the screen is off, which is how the app remains usable with a screen reader. Notifications are created on your device. Nothing is sent through a messaging service.
Run a foreground service
Lets beacon detection keep working while the screen is off, so you are not required to hold the phone in front of you for a whole walk. While this is active you will see an ongoing notification saying the app is listening; that notification is how you can tell it is running.
Internet
Used to download the trail information, landmark photos and map images described below.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app saves stays on your device:

  • Your settings — whether you have seen the welcome screen, whether Simplified Text is on, and whether landmark announcements are on.
  • A cache of the downloaded trail information and landmark photos, so the app still works where there is no mobile signal.
  • A cache of map images you have already viewed, for the same reason.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. You can also clear it at any time through Android's Settings, under this app's storage.

Information sent to other services

The app has no server of its own and no account system, so there is nothing to sign in to and no profile of you anywhere. It does request files over the internet, and as with any web request the service on the other end can see the IP address making it:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts the trail information and landmark photos at trails.warringtoneac.org. Governed by GitHub's privacy statement.
  • OpenStreetMap — supplies the map images. Governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Neither request includes your location, your beacon detections, or any identifier the app has created for you. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or social-media components of any kind.

Children

These are public parks and the app is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children specifically, and it collects no personal information from anyone regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.
