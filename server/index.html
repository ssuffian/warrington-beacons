How it works

From trailhead to the next discovery.

The app works as a map, a trail guide and an optional hands-free companion. You decide how much guidance you want.

01

Pick a place

Choose Lions Pride Park or the US-202 trail area and browse what is nearby.

02

Choose a route

Preview the trail, select a direction and begin from the closest stop.

03

Walk and explore

Read at your pace or let the app announce supported landmarks as you arrive.

Two ways to explore

Close to home. Full of surprises.

From an all-ages park loop to a longer greenway connection, Warrington Talking Trails helps you see familiar places differently.

Warrington Township

Lions Pride Park

Paved and crushed-stone trails, accessible play, nature signs and community gathering spaces.

US-202 to Bradford Dam

Creekside connection

A longer route through meadows, wetlands and habitat restoration areas with stories along the way.

Explore your way

Useful with or without beacon alerts.

Every landmark remains available from the map and trail lists. Bluetooth guidance adds timely prompts, while the core experience remains easy to browse anywhere.
