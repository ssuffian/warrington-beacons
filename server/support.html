Warrington Talking Trails Support

Warrington Talking Trails is a self-guided guide to Lions Pride Park and the US-202 to Bradford Dam Trail. It is an independent application and is not an official Warrington Township application.

Getting started

  • Allow location access while using the app so it can show your position and detect trail beacons.
  • Turn on Bluetooth to receive nearby-landmark alerts.
  • Open the app with an internet connection once to download and cache trail information and photos.

If trail information does not load

Confirm that the device is online, reopen the app, and tap Try Again. Once the first download succeeds, cached information can be used when the trail has poor service.

If nearby landmarks are not detected

Check that Bluetooth is on and that Location Services are enabled for Warrington Talking Trails. Beacon availability can also vary if trail hardware is being maintained. All landmarks remain available through the map, search, and Trail Tours screens.
