Warrington Talking Trails can be fully reviewed without visiting the trails or detecting a physical beacon. Open Trail Tours, select a trail, and tap Start Tour to review the guided-tour experience. Landmarks can also be opened from Park Map search.

Location is used to show the user's position and to detect nearby iBeacon landmarks. Bluetooth/iBeacon and location observations are processed on the device and are not sent to the developer. If permissions are declined, maps, search, landmark information, and tours remain available.

The app has no login, purchases, advertising, analytics, or tracking. It is an independent application and not an official Warrington Township application.
